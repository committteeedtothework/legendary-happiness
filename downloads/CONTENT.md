## Playable additions

- Wood families and original material palettes: spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, bamboo, crimson, warped and poplar materials where supported. Logs have axis placement; axes strip supported logs. Existing oak/custom wood remains. Birch and pine now produce birch/spruce planks; other older recipe indices and outputs remain stable.
- Stone, deepslate, tuff, calcite, mud, sandstone, polished/chiseled/cracked masonry, Nether-style stone, quartz, copper variants, mineral/storage blocks, dyed wool/concrete/terracotta/glass and compatible decoration families.
- Slabs place top/bottom and merge into doubles, stairs orient and form neighbor corners, fences/walls/panes connect, gates/trapdoors toggle, new door families keep linked upper/lower halves, ladders support climbing, carpets remain shallow, and chains/lanterns/pots/cores have separate shapes. Collision, rays, outlines and world meshes share shape definitions. Placement rejects actual solid intersections with players. Double slabs return two slabs when mined; doors drop one item.
- Original 16-pixel material motifs and cutouts, family geometry, proportioned isometric icons and shared held/F5/dropped models. New melee equipment uses the existing rigid palm grip. Ordinary item sprites remain flat; blocks and modeled weapons retain depth. Existing pistols, Devin Sword, skins and creature designs remain.
- Copper and Netherite tools/armor, chainmail where applicable, Golden/Copper/Netherite spear additions, a modeled mace, bows, crossbows, arrows and a main-hand shield. New equipment reads reference durability, stack limits and available combat/armor attributes. Mace hits receive a simplified falling smash bonus and reset fall accounting on a successful smash. Bow/crossbow charging consumes real arrows; crossbows persist their loaded state, arrows use swept hits against creatures and stop at walls, and survival arrows hitting walls return one recoverable item. Main-hand shields block incoming projectiles within a front cone and wear down.
- Additional foods and ingredients feed actual recipes and existing hunger/saturation. Shears can shear Woollings and harvest new leaves. Sheep/woolling, pig, rabbit and chicken deaths use their corresponding foods. Previously sheared Woollings do not drop extra wool on death.
- Carrots, potatoes, beetroots and sweet berries can be planted and harvested. Crop ages save as block states. Watered, sky-exposed crops grow through four stages in loaded areas. Bone meal advances these crops; saplings can grow real wood/leaves after checking that a whole crown fits in loaded space. Wild flowers, seedlings and vegetable patches provide survival access.
- Barrels open a persistent 27-slot inventory. Smokers cook food and blast furnaces smelt supported ore/raw-metal inputs in five seconds, with the existing fuel/output/XP transaction rules. Stonecutters expose actual material choices in the recipe sidebar. Smithing tables require a Netherite upgrade template, diamond equipment and a Netherite ingot; upgrades consume inputs once and preserve enchantments and damage already taken. These stations use the shared crafting-grid UI rather than Java Edition's dedicated slot layout.
- Compass use reports world-spawn XYZ, clocks report the world hour, and a spyglass toggles a temporary zoom. Bedrock is available in Creative. Material-only ingredients are used by crafting; unsupported gadgets are deferred rather than inert catalog entries.

## Natural resources and access

Create a **new world (generator 16)** for new natural resources. Terrain heights, coast distances, villages, caves and existing custom quest locations retain the previous generation's overall layout. The new pass adds deepslate/deep-ore variants, separated redstone/mineral pockets, regional wood substitutions, flowers and vegetables. Existing saves retain generators 2–15 and gain the new crafting/Creative content without changing their natural terrain. Player edits override new natural generation.

Cinderworld is the game's existing custom dimension, **not a faithful Nether**. It gains quartz/gold deposits and small, uncommon Ancient Debris pockets below Y32. Smelt debris into scrap and craft Netherite using the imported recipe. Guarded Cinderworld structure chests gain upgrade templates and a seeded chance of a Heavy Core; existing flying Cinderworld threats can drop Breeze Rods. Those are **custom sources**, replacing unsupported Bastion/Trial Chamber/Breeze progression. The core + rod crafts a mace. Previously generated/edited/depleted containers are not used to manufacture repeat loot on reload.

All variants are accessible in Creative search. Some decorative materials/foods have crafting recipes but lack their reference-specific natural source, creature or harvest chain and therefore remain **Creative-only or require ingredients not yet obtainable in normal Survival**. The registry audit is not a claim of complete natural distribution or end-to-end obtainability for every new entry.

## Partial mechanics and differences

- Many material variants share original family textures/models. They do not reproduce Mojang UVs or sculpted models. The expansion assigns physical hardness using material rules and reference mining tags; hardness, blast resistance and exact loot probabilities are not a verified 1:1 table.
- Stair corners/fence connections cover ordinary builds, not every reference neighbor-update edge case. Waterlogging, all directional attachment rules, reference support-break propagation and exact fence/wall shapes remain partial or missing. Metal doors/hatches can be operated by hand in this build. Slabs/stairs do not displace/store fluid correctly in all cases.
- Copper oxidation/waxing, resin collection, glazed terracotta rotation, full mushroom-face states, new plant special abilities, hanging plants, wall-mounted variants and all reference lighting behavior are incomplete. Oxidized/waxed variants can be crafted/accessed where a supported recipe exists; they do not age automatically.
- Ordinary new plants use a shared four-stage growth/sapling system. They do not reproduce every crop's ages, light checks, random-tick probability, soil rules or tree species' complete silhouette. Tall flowers are compact cutouts rather than two-block reference plants. Sweet berries currently act as a planted food crop, not a complete damaging bush simulation. Woolling fleece regrowth is not implemented.
- New foods restore hunger/saturation; special golden/enchanted/poisonous food effects and eating containers/animation timings are incomplete. Crafting returns supported bucket/bottle remainders transactionally. Additional remainders and fully correct brewing remain deferred.
- Bow/crossbow charge/damage, shield timings and mace smash/knockback are simplified. Bow/crossbow enchanting, tipped/spectral/firework arrows, offhand equipment, melee shield blocking/axe disables and full reference spear combat are missing. Netherite item fire immunity and its knockback-resistance equipment modifier are not implemented. Player arrows are transient across save/realm travel like other existing non-trident projectiles; loaded crossbows persist. Co-op has no arrow PvP.
- The shared 3×3 station interface differs from Minecraft's station screens. Anvils, grindstones, looms, cartography/fletching functions and full campfire cooking are deferred. Ordinary wooden tools can still be crafted using the existing custom progression; the newer factual recipes supplement it.
- New default textures are original fallback art in all texture packs. Previously supplied pack PNGs remain unmodified; this update does not pretend that the supplied pack contains all new textures.
- Existing Survival simplifications, supported mob roster, custom quests/Stands, Cinderworld bosses, weather behavior and co-op restrictions remain described in FEATURES.md, AQUATIC.md and MULTIPLAYER.md. No entire new biome/creature/automation/dimension feature set is claimed here.

## Explicit remaining work

**Deferred, compatible content:** unimplemented species and their eggs; unsupported boats/carts/mounts and inventories; beds/signs/banners/candles and their full state/interaction families; archaeological items; new potion/status systems; bundles; painting placement; functional anvils/looms/grindstones and other workstations; sculk/trial/vault/creaking/resin/sulfur systems; complete redstone wiring/devices/automation; and the remainder identified by the audit. Many technical wall/age/attached states also require their owning feature. Deferred means unfinished, not excluded for being inconvenient.

**Excluded scope:** administrative/debug/invisible world-editor blocks/items and progression exclusively tied to the unsupported End dimension. The CSV records names and reasons. Nether-oriented resources are adapted to Cinderworld where implemented rather than being silently excluded.

## Launch, controls, compatibility

Download the matching **2.7.0 standalone HTML**, save it, and open it in Chrome/Edge on Windows 10/11. Singleplayer does not need a server, internet or ChatGPT. The Windows ZIP contains the same HTML and a launcher that requires installed Edge/Chrome. Native Windows launch is not tested on this Linux host. Browser profile/file-origin storage rules still apply: export backups before moving builds or profiles, and never open a new-content save with an older version.

Existing movement, mining, inventory and custom-weapon controls remain. Right-click operates stations/gates/doors and plants compatible crops; Shift bypasses block interaction to place against it. Hold/release right mouse to draw/shoot a bow or load a crossbow; right-click again to fire a loaded crossbow. Hold right mouse with the shield in your main hand to defend against front-facing projectiles. Use right-click with a spyglass to zoom; use it again, wait eight seconds or open a menu to stop. Creative has ten item categories plus global Search, Stands and Player Inventory & Crafting tabs. It displays a five-row, nine-column scrolling grid with a draggable scrollbar; left/right/shift-click keep their original stack behavior. Arrow keys navigate tabs. The recipe book still pages 60 recipes at a time.

Development: `npm ci`, `npm run dev`, `npm test`, `npm run test:e2e`, `npm run build`, `npm run build:standalone`. WebGL, chunk meshes/hidden-face removal/culling and the independent 20Hz simulation are retained. Chunk arrays now use 16-bit IDs; state rotations reuse a **32×128 tile atlas**, not one texture allocation or render object per block. Multiplayer uses protocol **4**, so use the matching new client and relay; older clients are rejected rather than truncating new block IDs. Public HTTPS relay operation remains optional; see MULTIPLAYER.md.

See VALIDATION.md for what was actually verified and the scope of those checks. The tests are not a zero-bug guarantee.
