# Giganto articulation and choreography — 2.8.13

This stage changes the Giganto encounter only. Overworld mechanics, textures, supplied assets, controls, player combat damage and world save formats are preserved. Earlier stages remain documented in GIGANTO-SWEEPS.md, GIGANTO-VOLLEY.md and GIGANTO-ASCENT.md.

## Working changes

Armor climbing follows actual weighted skin deformation, including torso, shoulders, elbows, thighs and knees. A separate cached CPU skeleton samples the same deterministic 12-second traversal motion as the visible rig. Every attachment stores a triangle index and barycentric weights; idle anchors, surface normals and earned checkpoints move with that triangle. Walking still traverses actual triangles and allows lateral input, wall jumps and release. Saved anchors resume on their moving triangle without replaying milestones. Legacy point-only anchors retain the existing validated projection fallback.

An indexed bounding-volume hierarchy refits to the deformed mesh. Bulk skinning precomputes bind-space vertices and influence arrays; it does not construct a matrix per vertex. A broad-phase check avoids full mesh updates while Sonic is far from reachable armor. Visible route beacons independently sample their triangles from the visible rig, including while the full collider is idle. Mesh counts and skin scales are retained. The contact system supports weighted joint deformation, rather than only moving an undeformed bind shell.

Giganto uses a gentle joint-animated traversal stance with moving arms, elbows, torso and legs, plus the existing moving root. W/S follow the surface uphill/downhill, A/D traverse, Shift boosts, Space jumps and E releases/grips. Uphill assistance uses the existing real-surface guide around armor lips while respecting lateral input. A short post-release collision grace prevents an overhang from projecting a wall jump immediately back onto the surface.

Normal Sonic now selects a dedicated ground-boost stride with a forward lean and both arms extended behind his shoulders. Its arm correction eases in/out and freezes during pause. Actual skinned shoe vertices provide ground clearance, preventing sole penetration while retaining the natural airborne frames of the stride and leaving the physical avatar unchanged. Airborne normal Sonic retains jump/fall animations; Super Sonic retains separate flight/boost flight. The physical avatar remains authoritative.

Partial runtime clips now inherit a sampled neutral idle pose instead of an unposed bind stance. Homing Shot endpoints and wall-running arm poses are corrected to avoid T-shaped transitions. Existing blend interruptions still start from the last displayed pre-IK pose; no duplicate character mesh or scale animation is added.

The third and fourth basic combo contacts now use distinct roundhouse and finishing kick clips, with contact-timed leg IK. The earned parry counter uses a dedicated Grand Slam kick rather than the generic punch clip. These replace poses without changing damage, attack windows, hit detection, costs or controls: heavy 62 at 0.18s, finisher 120 at 0.25s, counter 300/perfect counter 460 at 0.48s. Other abilities, boss phases and protected counters remain.

Cyloop completes its full revolution and settles at the closure point instead of travelling back to the earlier cast position. Q still cancels in place with responsive steering. In armor mode, voluntary descents and safe ground landings no longer force Sonic back to a higher checkpoint. Falling outside the arena and explicit retries still provide safety recovery. Rendering never writes avatar positions.

Runtime clips: **36 Sonic / 32 Giganto**; supplied GLBs remain unchanged.

## Fidelity and remaining work

These are original, reference-inspired animations, not extracted official animation clips or a frame-matched recreation. The supplied screenshots and existing project reference material remain the visual reference. Attempts to verify fresh online encounter guides were blocked or unavailable; no new exact-fidelity claim is made. Full official choreography, facial/finger performances, production cinematic direction and every official skill-tree move remain unfinished.

Legacy Ruins retains its earlier missed-platform checkpoint recovery. The traversal animation intentionally uses restrained limb motion so a complete foot-to-crown route stays playable; Giganto does not execute combat sweeps while Sonic is climbing. Contacts follow weighted deformation, but disconnected parts do not gain invisible bridge surfaces, and abruptly separating geometry can still require a jump. Uphill assistance is not general navigation over arbitrary new meshes. Combat hit regions remain the existing analytic approximations; foot IK respects anatomical reach and does not guarantee visible shoe contact across the entire forgiving damage radius. Music arrangements and cinematic timing retain the earlier documented approximations. No new recording, licensed animation or paid asset was acquired.
