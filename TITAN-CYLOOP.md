# DCSMCRAFT 2.8.6 — physical Cyloop, swats and power-boost audio

This update changes only the dedicated Giganto encounter, its new recording and packaging/docs. Ordinary-world gameplay/assets are preserved apart from the version label. Boss health, damage values, ability energy costs/cooldowns, portal, final Emerald, transformation, two phases, checkpoints, rewards and original-world return remain as in 2.8.5.

**E / Quick Cyloop** now moves the physical Sonic avatar around Giganto. Starting within 13 metres of a body surface spends the existing 45 energy and starts the existing 12-second cooldown. A continuous 0.35s outward approach leads to a complete 2.4s revolution at radius48m around the Titan's vertical axis, followed by a continuous 0.35s return. The full action lasts3.1s. Height is kept between24–100m for the orbital segment, blending from/to the activation position. The rendered skeleton, camera and saved avatar share the actual path; this is not a disconnected mesh orbit. Damage185 and the existing stagger occur once, when the full circle closes at2.75s. No damage is granted for a partial/cancelled circle, and out-of-range activation spends nothing. The separate stagger-immunity timer is retained.

During this explicit special, the path controls translation; mouse/Tab still control targeting/camera and **Q cancels immediately into a dodge**. Other attacks retain 2.8.5's free movement. The orbital segment has bounded protection from incidental arm/dorsal-beam contacts, with vulnerable approach/return sections. This prevents automatic rear lasers breaking the required circuit. A phase change at closure keeps Sonic at his current position instead of snapping him back; otherwise the return finishes smoothly. Quitting/reloading an in-progress orbit uses the existing protected combat recovery and never replays its damage/refunds its cost.

A blue body aura/corona, bright blue motion trail and progressively drawn circular energy ring follow the actual movement. The ring reaches360degrees only after Sonic does, then briefly fades. Existing particle controls apply; turning particles off hides the trail/ring without changing movement or damage. Lock-on frames the moving hero and giant together; Tab can switch to the manual camera. The supplied rig's Cyloop clip is retimed/banked along the path; it is custom motion using the existing assets, not official Frontiers motion capture.

**Giganto's aimed swat** is selected at close-range arm-attack opportunities while the player is reachable. It has a1.25s warning (difficulty-scaled), a0.55s active strike and3.2s recovery. It captures the player's position during its warning rather than tracking them unavoidably. Shared hand/forearm paths drive the imported arm IK and contact checks. An unguarded hit spends the existing9/14/20 difficulty-scaled rings once, cancels the player's pending strike, adds an outward54m/s/upward14m/s launch and decays over0.65s. Air steering remains available; the force is not immediately erased by normal movement smoothing. The original1.25s hit immunity prevents repeated same-swat damage. Parry/counter, timed dodge, vertical escape or moving outside the captured sweep remain valid. Unreachable high/low positions do not trigger a global swat. Swat impacts add a directed burst/ripple and bounded camera shake; existing swing/slap/beam/grab behavior remains.

**Shift + movement / power boost** now plays the user-supplied Sonic Frontiers Power Boosting Sound Effect.mp3 through Web Audio. The complete original MP3 is embedded byte-for-byte in the standalone HTML and executable. It decodes once lazily; the original intro plays on boost activation, with the3.0–8.8s section sustained while the button is held. Release/standing still/pause/cinematics/disabled encounter audio/master mute stops the effect with a55ms fade. Exit disposes its buffer/nodes, and delayed decoding cannot start audio after leaving. At most two transient voices exist during rapid retrigger/fade. This is an SFX channel, independent of the Music toggle; master volume and encounter Audio apply. Actual horizontal and vertical boost movement consumes boost energy; standing with Shift held is silent. Cyloop retains its separate ability cue, not an overlapping second boost recording.

The file's metadata identifies J0LLY F3LL0W as uploader/artist. Sound rights remain with Sega and the respective rightsholders; no separate redistribution license accompanied the upload. It is outside the project MIT license. See public/audio/giganto/BOOST-NOTICE.txt and boost-manifest.json. Existing character meshes, textures, named GLB clips and both music recordings remain byte-identical; no paid service was used. Source rebuilds reconstruct the new embed with scripts/embed-titan-boost.mjs through the normal npm hooks.

Remaining limits from TITAN-COMBAT.md apply: stylized PBR scenery, custom choreography, analytic ellipsoid/capsule contacts and approximate elbows/hand contacts. The swat reuses the existing authored arm clips with a new shared targeted IK path, not a newly motion-captured clip. The boost sustain is a selected loop region rather than a supplied separate studio loop/stem. Tests, timing measurements, save/reward/return checks and platform limitations are in VALIDATION.md.
