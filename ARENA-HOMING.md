# Homing Attack · 2.16.0

Energy is removed from Giganto and Wyvern. Sonic and Super Sonic can boost and use abilities without energy costs, regeneration, reserves or an energy HUD. Climbing and the post-victory return flight follow the same rule. Existing saves ignore their old arena energy value. Rings still sustain Super Sonic; ring drain, boss health, damage values and rewards are unchanged. Quick Cyloop's hit-earned charges and Phantom Rush's combo meter remain separate combat systems. Overworld Stand energy is unchanged.

A successful normal combat parry now starts Homing Attack in both fights. Wyvern no longer warps Sonic or snaps the camera to a destination. Sonic briefly winds up, curls into a golden form, travels to a reachable Titan body surface, strikes on physical contact and rebounds into flight. The dash uses the existing swept body/scenery collision checks. An obstructed approach ends without a remote hit. Dodge or switching lock-on cancels it. A buffered attack after a parry follows the homing contact; no queued attack can hit before arrival.

**X** manually uses Homing Attack; controller and touch Homing Attack bindings remain unchanged. Manual targeting reaches 70 arena units. The post-parry approach can close up to 1,400 units across Wyvern's large chase area. Windup is 0.15 seconds (0.22 after a parry), minimum contact travel is 0.16 seconds, recovery is 0.22 seconds, and a blocked dash aborts after 0.22 seconds without progress. These are authored tuning values, not measured Frontiers constants. Ordinary Super Sonic cooldowns remain removed. Animation contact and recovery still prevent duplicate damage from repeated input.

Homing Attack emits its launch sound when the windup finishes. Damage and impact effects occur at swept contact, once per attack. Gold effects remain gold; ordinary Super Sonic movement does not gain a blue trail. The original character rig returns after the curl. Pausing stops the approach clock. Reloading safely discards an unfinished attack, following the existing transient-attack save behavior; it does not warp Sonic or award pending damage.

## Reference and limits

Checked SEGA's official [Action overview](https://asia.sega.com/SonicFrontiers/en/action/) and its combat screenshots, including [Sonic's airborne combat](https://asia.sega.com/SonicFrontiers/en/action/images/slider02-02.jpg) and [Super Sonic against Giganto](https://asia.sega.com/SonicFrontiers/en/action/images/slider02-09.jpg). The site describes high-speed movement around opponents and chained combos. Screenshots establish presentation, not exact input windows or production animation curves. The project's prior user-supplied [moveset reference](https://youtu.be/ao6-F7-WP8k) and [Wyvern recording analysis](WYVERN-VIDEO.md) remain context.

This build adapts the lock-on cue, curled approach, readable contact, recoil and combo follow-through. Automatic post-parry homing is the requested DCSMCRAFT behavior; it is not claimed as an exact reconstruction of Frontiers' button sequence. It aims for the nearest reachable visible Titan body region, rather than recreating every authored face-approach camera or animation. Supplied rigs, original procedural poses and analytic collision shapes remain. No official animation clips, new commercial media or assets were downloaded into the game. Some external guide pages were unavailable, so exact frame timings and one-to-one fidelity were not verified.
