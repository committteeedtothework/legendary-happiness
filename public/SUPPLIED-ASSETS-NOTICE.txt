DCSMCRAFT is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mojang or
Microsoft. See supplied-pack/manifest.json in the source/web build for the
individual source paths, SHA-256 hashes, frame selection and color transforms.
The original Renewed, Soft and Classic packs remain available. No Minecraft
code, models, sounds, fonts, text, shaders or gameplay data were imported.
