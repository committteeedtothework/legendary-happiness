# Aquatic Voyages — 2.5.0

Playable ocean expansion. Source: Mojang's [Update Aquatic Phase Two is out today](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/update-aquatic-phase-two-out-today), Tom Stone, 10 July 2018. That announcement describes **Bedrock**, including Switch and optional Education features. DCSMCRAFT continues to reference **Java Edition 1.20.4**; it does not switch its whole mechanics baseline to 2018 Bedrock. Code, new pixel artwork, models and synthesized voices are original.

Create a **new world** for generator 15's habitats and ocean structures. Existing generators 2–14 keep their terrain and edits. New items, crafting, simulation and Creative eggs work in existing worlds; natural sea-life spawning also works around old water. The supplied resource pack, unique weapons, Stands, portals and both supplied claim recordings are retained. See README for launch, saves and controls.

## Additional ocean dependencies added

Nine ocean climate/depth variants retain connected coastlines: warm, lukewarm, normal, cold, frozen and the four applicable deep variants. Kelp forests, seagrass, five coral palettes, sea pickle groups, magma/soul vents and packed/blue-ice formations diversify the seabed. Sparse seeded wreck/ruin layouts have variant hulls/walls, supply chests, usable coordinate treasure maps and buried Heart-of-the-Sea loot. Removed/depleted containers remain removed/depleted on reload.

Cod, salmon, tropical fish, pufferfish, squid, dolphins, turtles and Drowned have original voxel models, movement and voices; each has a Creative-only egg. Fish flop/die out of water; puffers inflate and poison at close range. Fish buckets preserve health and release one fish. Rods have bobbers/lines, bite windows, fish/shell loot, Lure/Luck of the Sea and durability. Ocean water fog varies with climate; local lamps and sea pickles light nearby water, respecting graphics controls. No copied Minecraft audio/assets were added to the original packs.

## Remaining scope and multiplayer limits

This is a playable expansion with the partial items above, not a 1:1 completion of Update Aquatic. General waterlogging/slabs/stairs, wall-mounted coral fans, full treasure-map artwork, exact spawning/lighting/navigation, complete potion/enchantment catalogs, ocean monuments/Guardians/Elder Guardians and reference loot tables remain future work. Phase-one systems not mentioned in the source are not claimed complete. Ocean Journal records are world-wide in co-op.

Survival co-op uses host authority for water edits, creatures, loot, containers, brewing, equipment and tridents; clients must use the matching 2.5.0 relay. Creative guest utility actions remain restricted by the existing beta (including boats and some new utilities); singleplayer Creative has all content. The host's loaded region controls simulation; independent guest dimensions/PvP remain unavailable. Native Windows launch is unverified on Linux; exact standalone HTML is browser-tested over local HTTP with external requests blocked, because managed Chromium does not allow file navigation. Save/export a backup before changing versions.
