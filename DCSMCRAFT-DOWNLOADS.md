DCSMCRAFT 2.2.1 — Local Discs & Creative Stands

Pixel/stone button styling is applied to the new disc and Stand controls. YouTube URL/iframe/SDK playback has been removed. MP3s remain local, outside world backups and relay packets; another PC/profile must relink them. Hosts insert/eject in co-op; guest listening is personal and unsynchronized. 396 core tests and 12 distinct browser scenarios pass, including genuine offline MP3 decoding and persistence, transfer/drop consistency, Creative co-op permissions, original gathering/crafting, Survival trials, held-item clipping, supplied textures and actual local HTTPS/WSS.
