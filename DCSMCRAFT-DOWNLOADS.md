DCSMCRAFT 2.1.0 — Weather, Sound & Grip

Complete centered crosshair; fitted handle/pistol/pinch/open-palm block grips, camera near-plane safeguards and F5 flat-item repair. Persistent, collidable eight-layer snow accumulation, melting, roof cover and co-op block synchronization. Rare seeded tornado tracks cross the warning area with dense funnels, flying debris, exposed-player pull/lift/damage and creature/drop effects; roofs shelter players and buildings are preserved. Original sound redesign: 27 creature identities with distinct idle/attack/hurt/death calls, 45 layered effect profiles, nine Foley materials, spatial filtering/wall muffling and lazy cached variants. Existing worlds, generator 12, recipes, weapon abilities and Stand ownership are retained. Mechanics reference: Minecraft Java 1.20.4, partial baseline parity.

396 core tests and 15 selected browser scenarios pass. Exact standalone HTML tested with outside requests blocked. Native Windows execution remains unverified on this Linux build host; Edge or Chrome is required. The executable embeds the tested HTML byte-for-byte. Snow accumulates locally over minutes, caps at one block, and uses simplified temperature/light rules. Tornadoes are custom local hazards, not a destructive structural simulation. Sound is original synthesis rather than imported game recordings. Source, assets, dependencies, controls, launch instructions, validation and known limitations are included in the ZIPs.
