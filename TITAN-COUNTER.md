# Titan chase and counters · 2.13.0

Wyvern turns away during transformation and stays aligned with its canyon route during pursuit. Sonic starts beyond its tail rather than inside its body. Lock-on boost guides Sonic above and around the tail; steering overrides that assistance. Idle pursuit retains world position. The pre-fight ribbon supports acceleration, braking, reverse movement, smooth lane changes and jumping. Its rendered deck and collision path use the same terrain-aware elevation, including clearance for all three lanes.

Reflecting one missile now immediately blinks Sonic to the front of Wyvern and opens a 12-second damage window in either phase. The move includes the finger flick, short slow motion, departure/arrival rings, a camera reset outside the body, and brief contact protection. Basic attacks and specials can start immediately; no mandatory claw counter or automatic Grand Slam interrupts the opening. Missed missiles can still provoke an optional close claw or bite; evading it returns to pursuit without requiring a parry. Each parry press still reflects only once. The early window is 650 / 550 / 450 ms on Easy / Normal / Hard, or 300 ms with Strict Parry; contact also allows 140 ms of late input. Holding beyond the window remains vulnerable. These intentionally forgiving rules follow the requested variation rather than the original game's held-parry sequence.

Base Sonic curls into one blue spinning form during jumps in either arena. The imported rig is hidden once the new form fully covers its silhouette, then returns on landing or transformation. The blue aura and three bounded arcs obey Particles; the solid curled form remains visible with effects disabled. Super Sonic receives no blue trail. Existing boost and other supplied recordings remain unchanged; no new jump recording was added.

Wyvern's timing prompts accept a wider portion of the shrinking ring. Slow boss-relative camera arcs and close jaw shots replace fixed-side framing; hands brace toward the supplied jaw joints without scaling the arms. Thrown and finishing poses use the same heading as the boss. This is original procedural choreography, not imported official animation. The supplied rigs and their previously documented limitations remain.

Both arenas now default to a 15-minute day cycle, adjustable from 10 to 60 minutes. Wyvern's dawn progresses over half the chosen cycle. Persisted sky clocks preserve old save lighting while slowing future progress; victory still freezes the defeated arena's weather and daylight. Encounter music uses its existing independent timing and cue regions. Pausing, checkpoint retries, exit and the original-world return use the existing systems.

Wyvern uses soft PCF shadows, texel-snapped tracking and 1K / 2K / up-to-4K shadow maps. The arena restores the Overworld shadow filter on exit. Rocks and ruins share deterministic render and collision placements across quality settings; the player and camera use conservative obstacle volumes. Both terrain LODs share the collision height field. The enlarged central clearing protects the static pre-fight boss and tail from terrain intersection.

Controls: W/S run or brake on the ribbon; A/D steer; Shift boost; Space jump. In Super form, WASD fly, Space/Ctrl ascend/descend, RMB parry, Q dodge, Tab target. Existing controller and mobile bindings remain and receive the updated prompts.

Health, attack damage, music regions, items, rewards, Overworld gameplay and public assets are unchanged from 2.12.0. Existing arena saves retain health fraction, pickups and return state. Combat checkpoints restore Wyvern's away-facing orientation; the saved player position is not snapped to a new combat location on reload.

Validation results are recorded in VALIDATION.md. Analytic collision volumes approximate the supplied meshes: an exhaustive guarantee against every possible surface intersection is not claimed. The curled jump is original geometry inspired by the supplied image, rather than an official Sonic asset. Native Windows, physical controllers and iOS Safari need hardware testing; Chromium software rendering does not establish desktop or phone frame rates.
